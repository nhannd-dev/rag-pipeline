# How to Use the MindMeister App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500005889381-How-to-Use-the-MindMeister-App
Last updated: 2026-10-02T18:17:46Z

Mind maps are highly visual, engaging ways to organize and communicate your thoughts. These can be created with MindMeister, then displayed and shared using OptiSigns.

You can create MindMeister app with OptiSigns.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| To use the MindMeister app, you'll need to be on an OptiSigns [Standard plan or above](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing). |

### Let's jump in and get started:

On the OptiSigns portal, go to Files/Assets, Click on "App" and add MindMeister to your Account.

![Add App dialog on Files/Assets with MindMeister typed in the search box and the MindMeister app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56030878787731)

Then go to MindMeister. We'll need to get an Embed Code:

1. Click the "Share" button on the bottom right of your MindMeister. Then click "Publishing setting".

![MindMeister map with the Share menu open and Publishing settings highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500008067421)

2. Then click "Embed map"

![MindMeister Publishing settings window with the Embed map link highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500008067461)

3. Copy the embed code. Then paste it to the Embed Code section in the MindMeister app within OptiSigns.

![MindMeister Embed Wizard showing frame size settings and the embed code to copy](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500007877422)

**Note**: You can set the Frame width and Frame height in your embed code.

Then come back to OptiSigns and enter in your MindMeister info.

![MindMeister app settings with Name, Embed Code and Update Interval, and a live preview of the mind map](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56030878794003)

- Name: Name of your MindMeister app, this is the name of the wall in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Embed Code: Paste your MindMeister embed code. Once pasted, you'll be able to Preview what it will look like on your screen. The Preview can also be adjusted between Landscape and Portrait. You can adjust the Frame width and height within this code to get it to fit exactly as you'd like to.
- Update Interval: default is 10 minutes. This means the app will refresh the link every 10 mins for any changes in your presentation. You can also input an interval here on how often you want to refresh the content.

Click Save.

---

### That's all!

You have created your MindMeister.
You can change it any time by click on it in the Files/Assets tab.

You can assign the newly created wall to your screen by going to Screens, click Edit screens and assign the MindMeister to screens that you want.

You can put the created MindMeister in a Playlist or Schedule too.
