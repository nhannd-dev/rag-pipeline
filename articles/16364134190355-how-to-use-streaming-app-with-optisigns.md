# How to use Streaming App with OptiSigns

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/16364134190355-How-to-use-Streaming-App-with-OptiSigns
Last updated: 2026-09-14T16:05:11Z

Putting a Streaming video on your digital signs screens is easy with OptiSigns.

## Let's jump in and get started:

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891-OptiSigns-Getting-Started-Guide).

Then log on to our portal: [https://app.optisigns.com/](http://app.optisigns.com/)

Go to **Files/Assets**, then click on **Apps**. Choose the **Streaming App:**

![Files/Assets with the Apps button and the Add App dialog, arrows on Files/Assets, Apps and Streaming App](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55401401563155)

Enter your Streaming App information:

![Streaming App dialog with Name and URL filled in and the Advanced section expanded](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55401401564179)

- Name: Give your Streaming App a name. This is for displaying in your asset list only.
- URL: Put your streaming URL here.

**Advanced:**

- Render Engine: First choose your device (FireStick Normal, FireStick 4K, or Other). Then select the Normal, CrossWalk, or Advanced Webview on your type of device.
  - Require gesture: Set webview for website with video to require touch to play
  - Simulate touch after load: Set webview for website video require touch to play (If you experiencing video auto play issue with your website, try combination of this setting and Required user gesture)

Click **Save**.
After Saving, you can Preview the video to verify.

---

## That's all! Congratulations!

You have added a Streaming App.

You can assign the newly created Streaming App asset to your screen by going to Screens, click Edit screens and assign the app to screens that you want.

You can put the Streaming App inside a Playlist or Schedule, as well.
