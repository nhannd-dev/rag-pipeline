## Setting Up a Simple Clock App

On the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets → Apps**.

![Files/Assets left panel with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54422800153491)

Select **Simple Clock:**

![Add App results showing Simple Clock, World Clock, CountDown and CountUp](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54612347362963)

Set up your Simple Clock app:

![Simple Clock dialog: Name, Clock Type and Theme on the left, live clock preview on the right](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54612356601747)

- **Name -** Name of your assets. This is for organizational purposes and will not be displayed on your screens.
- **Clock Type -** Choose your type of clock. Pick the one that you like best! There are six options:
  - **Classic Digital**
  - **Modern Digital**
  - **Classic Analog**
  - **Modern Analog**
  - **Minimalist**
  - **Kinetic**
- **Theme -** Choose between **Light** and **Dark** theme, or set it to Auto (6 AM/PM), which will automatically change between the two in the morning and evening.
- **Landscape / Portrait -** Switch the Preview between Landscape, Portrait, and Custom.

<a id="ThemeandClock"></a>

### Theme and Clock Settings

The actual Theme and Clock Settings will vary depending on whether your clock display is digital or analog.

The remaining options live in two collapsible sections, **Clock Settings** and **Theme Settings**. Click either one to expand it. Which sections appear — and what’s inside them — depends on the Clock Type you chose.

<a id="Digital"></a>

#### Digital Clocks

![Clock Settings with Time Format, Show Seconds, Show Date and Date Format](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685871185043)

- **Time Format -** How you want the time to be formatted on your digital clock.
  - **Show Seconds -**Choose whether or not to show seconds.
  - **Show Date -**Choose whether or not to display the date.
- **Date Format -**If **Show Date** is turned on, you can customize the way the date is formatted here.

  ![The Date Format dropdown highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685871232147)
- **Time Font Size -** Choose the font size for the time display.
- **Date Font Size -** Choose the font size for the date. This option only appears if **Show Date** is turned on above.
- **Text Color -** Choose the Text color for the digital clock.
- **Background Color -** Choose the Background color for the display.

![Theme Settings with Text Color and Background Color highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685881353619)

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Using Modern Digital, there are 6 Color Presets: Neon Cyberpunk, Minimalist Zen, Retro Digital, Elegant Classic, Gaming RGB, and Nature Organic.  The Background colors of these Presets cannot have their gradients changed. Modern Digital also has its own **Font Family**, **Font Size**, and **Text Animation** options. |

<a id="Analog"></a>

#### Analog Clocks

Analog clock types have only a **Theme Settings** section.

![The Theme dropdown showing Light, Dark and Auto](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685871290131)

![Analog Theme Settings with arrows on the Show Numbers and Show Tick Marks toggles](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685915349395)

- **Show Numbers -** Choose whether or not to show numbers on the analog clock display.
- **Show Tick Marks -** Choose whether or not to show tick marks on the analog clock display.
- **Dial Color** - Change the dial color on the analog clock display.
- **Hour Hand Color -** Change the color of the hour hand on the analog clock display.
- **Minute Hand Color -** Change the color of the minute hand on the analog clock display.
- **Second Hand Color -** Change the color of the second hand on the analog clock display.
- **Clock Face Background -** Change the background color of the clock face.
- **Background Color -** Change the background color of the entire display outside the clock face.

Modern Analog uses a different set: **Outer Face Color**, **Inner Face Color**, **Clock Border Color**, and **Tick Mark Color** in place of Dial Color and Clock Face Background.

Once you've configured the app the way you want, hit **Save**. Save stays greyed out until you've entered a **Name**.

## Deploying a Simple Clock App

You can deploy your new Simple Clock app as an individual asset, or as part of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573-How-to-Create-and-Use-the-Split-Screen-App).

To get your new Simple Clock asset to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click the screen you want to assign it to.

![Screens list with the Edit button on a screen row highlighted, shown magnified below](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685871536531)

This brings up the **Edit Screen** menu:

![The Edit Screen window with Device Name, Content Type, Selected Playlist and Orientation](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54685881469459)

Here, select **Asset** under Content type. Then, select your created Simple Clock Asset:

![Edit Screen with the Selected Asset row highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54612367478291)

Now hit **Save**. Your Simple Clock asset will now display on screen.

You can also deploy it as part of a split screen, allowing you to show other assets at the same time. See how in our [Split Screen app article.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573-How-to-Create-and-Use-the-Split-Screen-App) It can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule.
