# How to Use the Social Mix App

Last updated: 2026-10-15T21:07:28Z

### In this article, we'll show how to combine posts from your social media apps into a single wall on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Creating a Social Mix App](#CreatingASocialMixApp)
  - [Advanced](#Advanced)
- [Deploying a Social Mix App](#DeployingASocialMixApp)

With the Social Mix app, it's possible to combine posts from your Facebook, Instagram and Instagram Business apps into a single social wall. Bring all of your social channels together on one screen, and let it keep up as new posts come in.

![Social Mix app showing one post at a time, with the photo beside the profile name and caption](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/26482818353171)

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYoullNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- At least one Facebook, Instagram or Instagram Business app already created in your **Files/Assets**. The Social Mix app pulls its posts from these apps
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))

## Creating a Social Mix App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461858175251)

Search for **Social Mix** and select the **Social Mix** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Social Mix, with the Social Mix app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461858175891)

The Social Mix app settings will open. Enter a **Name**, then add your sources. Next to **Add Source**, click the **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **Instagram Business** icon. A window opens listing your apps of that type. Click the one you want, then hit **Select**. Each source you add is listed under **Add Source**, with an **X** to remove it. To add another, click an icon under **Add more**.

![Select Facebook window with a Facebook app highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884193427)

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| Don't have a social app yet? Click **Create** in the same window to make one. |

Once your sources are added, fill in the rest of the settings:

- **Name** - The name of your Social Mix asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Add Source** - The Facebook, Instagram and Instagram Business apps whose posts appear in your wall.
- **Display Mode** - How your posts are laid out. Choose from **Social Kiosk View**, [**Single Post View**](https://social-player.optisigns.com/instagram/?asset_id=y89t3dg76jewc7nec), [**Social Wall View**](https://social-player.optisigns.com/instagram/?asset_id=ry2oyv3hqui83njer), **Timeline View**, **Bricks View**, **Modern View** or **Flip Card View**.
- **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**, or **Custom** to pick your own colors.
- **Speed** - How fast the app moves between posts. Choose **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or **Custom** to set your own speed.
- **Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** to set your own size.

![Social Mix app form with a Facebook source and an Instagram Business source added](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461925286163)

When **Theme** is set to **Custom**, color fields appear so you can set the colors of your wall, such as **Background Color** and **Text Color**. The fields shown depend on your **Display Mode**.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884194835)

When **Speed** is set to **Custom**, drag the slider to choose a speed from 1 to 20.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a slider from 1 to 20](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461925287571)

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing the post filter, scrolling strip, playlist and QR code options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884196243)

- **Filter Posts** - Choose **None**, **By Quantity** to show only the latest number of posts you set, or **By Time** to show only posts from the last number of days you set.
- **Only show posts with image** - Turn this on to hide posts without an image.
- **Scrolling Strip Font Size** - If you place the app in a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573) zone set up as a scrolling strip, keep **Default** or choose **Custom** to set the text size.
- **Continue Playing Content in Playlist** - On by default. In a Playlist, the app picks up where it left off each time it comes around, instead of starting again from the first post.
- **Show QR Code** - Turn this on to show a QR code linking to each post.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Social Mix app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**. It may take a few seconds for the app to fetch your posts.

![New Social Mix asset shown in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461925289875)

## Deploying a Social Mix App

To push your Social Mix app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884198035)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884199443)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Social Mix app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Social Mix asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56461884201107)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your social wall will now display on your screen.

Your Social Mix app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
