## What are OptiSigns Feeds?

OptiSigns Feeds are collections of prebuilt, fresh, and engaging content designed to enhance your office, workplace, or business. These feeds cover a wide range of topics and formats, providing dynamic and updated content to keep your digital signage interesting and relevant.

We provide a rich tapestry of categories to suit various environments:

- **Food and Travel:** Serves up culinary inspiration and destination highlights — a fit for restaurants, cafés, hotels, and break rooms.
- **Arts and Culture:** Brings art, architecture, and cultural landmarks to your screens for a touch of the extraordinary.
- **History:** Shares notable events, milestones, and this-day-in-history moments to spark curiosity in shared spaces.
- **Sports:** Keeps fans current with schedules, standings, and matchups from the leagues they follow.
- **Science:** Explores discoveries, natural phenomena, and how-it-works explainers to inform and engage.
- **Health and Wellness:** Offers practical health tips, wellness advice, and the latest in nutrition and fitness trends, tailored to inspire healthier lives.
- **Kids Zone:** Makes learning and waiting fun for children by transforming screens into platforms for education and entertainment.
- **Entertainment and Lifestyle:** Brings a mix of fun, news, and lifestyle tips to communal spaces, waiting areas, or retail locations.
- **Inspirational Quotes:** Offers daily motivation to uplift spirits and promote a positive mindset.
- **Relax-Reels:** Provides a visual escape with serene landscapes and calming imagery, ideal for stress relief.
- **Safety and Compliance:** Ensures a safe environment by keeping everyone informed about the latest protocols and standards.
- **Workplace Series:** Enhances the corporate atmosphere with content aimed at boosting productivity and positivity.

## How to Use Feeds

Log in to your account, and go to the [Files/Asset page](https://app.optisigns.com/app/assetManagement). Click **Feeds:**

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Feeds button highlighted in the left sidebar](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262874863635)

Start browsing and select which Feeds you want to use.

![The Feeds library: category list, featured feeds carousel and the full feed grid](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262874867091)

| Note: You can preview the Feeds content before using it. |
| --- |

![Create Feed Asset dialog previewing a feed, with the Next button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262874868243)

Press **"Next"**, and you can rename the Feed if you'd like, then press **"Save".**

Finally, push your Feeds to your screen! If you need help learning how to do this please visit this article: [**Push Contents to your Screens**](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18988049363859)**.**

| Once created, Feeds: |
| --- |
| - Can be used like any other assets in Playlist, Schedule or Split Screen. |
| - Can be cached to players local storage, so most Feeds will not depend on internet connection to play. |
| - That are curated YouTube feeds (Relax-Reel) **will require** internet connection to play. |

### Recommended Duration:

![Feed details panel with the Recommended Duration row highlighted, showing 30s](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262830272019)

Each feed has a recommended duration. This way it helps keep the screen fresh, not repetitive content.

| For example: |
| --- |
| The Warehouse - Slip, Trip, & Fall Prevention feed totals about 7 minutes across 13 items, and its recommended duration is 30s. In a playlist, the feed plays for 30s and then hands off to the next item. The next time the playlist loops back, the feed resumes where it left off and continues through its remaining content, until it reaches the end — then it starts over. |
