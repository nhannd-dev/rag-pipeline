With the CountDown app, it's possible to display a timer that counts down to any date and time you choose, then show a message of your choice when it reaches zero. Count down to a product launch, a holiday sale, or the start of an event, and let your screens build the anticipation.

## Creating a CountDown App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450475731603)

Search for **CountDown** and select the **CountDown** app.

![Add App dialog searched for CountDown, with the CountDown app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460395411)

The **Count Down** app settings will open. Any changes you make show up right away in the **Preview** on the right, which you can switch between **Landscape** and **Portrait**.

![Count Down app form with the Name, Text, End Time, Message, Timer Style and Theme fields filled in](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460396435)

Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your CountDown asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Text** - The title displayed above the timer, such as **Countdown to Our Grand Opening**.
- **End Time** - The date and time the timer counts down to.
- **Message** - The message displayed when the countdown reaches zero, such as **We're Open!**
- **Timer Style** - How the timer looks. Choose from **Flip**, **Basic**, **Ring** or **Slide**.
- **Days only** - Check this box to show only the number of days, without hours, minutes and seconds.
- **Theme** - A preset color scheme for the timer. Choose from **Light**, **Dark**, **Sky**, **Carrot**, **Flamingo**, **Forest**, **Grape**, **Meadow**, **Neapolitan**, **Pineapple**, **Urban** or **Watermelon**.

Click the **End Time** field to open the calendar. Pick a date, set the hour, minute and AM/PM, then hit **Apply**.

![End Time calendar with a date selected and the Apply button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460398099)

<a id="ThemeSettings"></a>

### Theme Settings

Click **Theme Settings** to fine-tune the look of your timer:

- **Primary Color** - The background color. Enter a hex code or click the swatch to use the color picker.
- **Secondary Color** - The text color. Enter a hex code or click the swatch to use the color picker.
- **Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** and enter your own size.
- **Transparency** - Drag the slider to make the app's background more transparent.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Changing any Theme Setting switches **Theme** to **Custom**. |

![Theme Settings section expanded, showing Primary Color, Secondary Color, Font Size and Transparency](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460399123)

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| For full transparency when using the app in a Designer layout or a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573), set **Background Image** to **Transparent** in **Edit Screen** for your device. |

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded with Recurring checked, showing Repeat Every, Ends, Show Date, Background Image and Language](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460400531)

- **Recurring** - Check this box to restart the countdown on a regular schedule. Set **Repeat Every** to a number of **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**, then choose when it **Ends**:
- **Never** - The timer keeps repeating.
- **On** - The timer stops repeating on a date you choose.
- **After** - The timer stops repeating after a set number of times.
- **Show Date** - Check this box to show the date on the timer, then pick a **Date Format**.
- **Background Image** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** and hit **Choose** under **Choose Photo** to use an image from your Files/Assets. A custom background image replaces the background color.
- **Language** - The language for the timer labels and date. Choose from **English**, **Deutsch (German)**, **Español (Spanish)**, **Français (French)**, **Italiano (Italian)**, **Nederlands (Dutch)**, **Português (Portuguese)**, **Svenska (Swedish)** or **中文 (Simplified Chinese)**.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a CountDown app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

![New CountDown asset shown in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450475741331)

## Deploying a CountDown App

To push your CountDown app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450475747603)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450460409875)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your CountDown app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the CountDown asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56450475750803)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your CountDown timer will now display on your screen.

Your CountDown app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
