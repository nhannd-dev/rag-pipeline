# How to Use the Eventbrite Events App

### In this article, we'll show how to display your upcoming Eventbrite events on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

With the Eventbrite Events app, it's possible to display upcoming events from Eventbrite on your digital signs. Promote your own events, or those of a venue or organizer you love, complete with dates, venues, ticket prices and a QR code viewers can scan to get tickets.

![Eventbrite Events app showing an event poster with its date, venue, ticket price and a QR code to register](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498383266579)

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYoullNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- Either an Eventbrite account with an organizer profile, or the public Eventbrite link of the event, organizer or venue you want to show
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))

## Creating an Eventbrite Events App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498354191635)

Search for **Eventbrite** and select the **Eventbrite Events** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Eventbrite, with the Eventbrite Events app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498391528979)

You'll be asked how to load your events. Choose one:

- **Continue with Eventbrite** - Sign in to your Eventbrite organizer account and show your own events.
- **Use public events** - Show any organizer's, venue's or event's events using their public Eventbrite link. No Eventbrite account is needed.

![Eventbrite Events dialog with the Continue with Eventbrite and Use public events options, Use public events highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498876968083)

<a id="ContinueWithEventbrite"></a>

### Continue with Eventbrite

Click **Continue with Eventbrite** and sign in to Eventbrite in the window that opens, then approve access. The app settings will open with your Eventbrite connection shown at the top. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Eventbrite Events asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Organization** - The Eventbrite organization whose events are shown, if your account has more than one.
- **Venue** - Show events at all of your venues, or pick one venue.

An **Advanced** section is also available with this option:

- **Include unlisted (private link) events** - Check this box to also show events you've set as unlisted on Eventbrite.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| The Eventbrite account you sign in with needs an organizer profile. If it doesn't have one, you'll see **Couldn't connect Eventbrite**. Set up an organizer profile on eventbrite.com, then try again. |

<a id="UsePublicEvents"></a>

### Use Public Events

Click **Use public events**. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Eventbrite Events asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Eventbrite link** - The public Eventbrite link to show. Paste an **event** link to show that one event, or an **organizer** or **venue** link to show everything they have coming up, such as **https://www.eventbrite.com/o/your-venue-12345**.

![Eventbrite Events form with the Name and Eventbrite link fields filled in, and the Display section below](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498376547091)

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Eventbrite has no "events near me" search, so the app shows whatever the link you paste points to. |

## Display

The **Display** section controls which events are shown and how they look.

- **Layout** - How your events are presented. See below.
- **Show events in the next** - How far ahead to look for events: **7 days**, **14 days**, **30 days** (the default) or **90 days**.
- **Seconds per slide** - How long each event is shown, from 5 to 20 seconds. Only available with the **Event Marquee** layout.
- **Number of events** - How many events rotate through the screen, from 3 to 8. Each layout shows as many as fit at once and pages through the rest. Available with every layout except **Event Marquee**.
- **Theme** - **Dark** (the default), **Light**, or **Custom**.
- **Show ticket price** - Shows each event's ticket price, or **Free**. On by default.
- **Show QR code to buy tickets** - Shows a QR code that opens the event's Eventbrite page. On by default.

![Layout dropdown open, listing Event Marquee, Neighborhood Board, Right Now and Going Fast](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498399273363)

There are four layouts:

- **Event Marquee** - One event at a time, with its poster, date, time, venue, price and QR code, plus a preview of the next event. This is the default.
- **Neighborhood Board** - A board of upcoming event cards, each with its own price and QR code.
- **Right Now** - The current time, the next event up with its QR code, and a list of the events coming up after it.
- **Going Fast** - Spotlights one event at a time under a **Going Fast** banner.

Set **Theme** to **Custom** to choose your own **Background color**, **Text color** and **Accent color**. Enter a hex code or click the swatch to use the color picker.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the Background color, Text color and Accent color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498377036947)

![Eventbrite Events Event Marquee layout in the Light theme](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498407790483)

![New Eventbrite Events asset shown in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498355272595)

## Deploying an Eventbrite Events App

To push your Eventbrite Events app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498408052499)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498400184211)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Eventbrite Events app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Eventbrite Events asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56498392863379)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your events will now display on your screen.

Your Eventbrite Events app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
