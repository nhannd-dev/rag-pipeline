### In this article, we'll explain how to use the News app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)

Our News app allows you to display news on your digital signs from a wide range of topics and regions. Such topics include Business, Technology, World, Politics, and Sports, and you can specify the country or region to focus your news app on.

## Creating a News App Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to **Files/Assets**, click on **Apps**:

![OptiSigns portal with the Files/Assets tab and the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264333071635)

Click **News**:

![Add App dialog searched for News, with the News app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264358549395)

Enter your News Wall App information:

![News Wall editor: Name, Country, Category, Display Mode, Theme, Speed and Font Size beside a live preview](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264333073043)

- Name: Name of your News app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Country: Currently we support United States and United Kingdom news.
- Category: Select the region and category that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want.
- Display Mode: Select Single Post View,Social Kiosk View, Social Wall View, or Timeline View. Click on the links to see a live example.
- Theme: Select Light or Dark Theme. You can also customize your background color if you select Custom.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the Background Color and Text Color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264333073555)

- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, showing the 1-to-20 speed slider](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264333073939)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the News Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![Advanced expanded: Date and Time, Scrolling Strip Font Size, Continue Playing Content in Playlist](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264358551443)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date and Time set to Custom, showing the custom date format field](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264358552339)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the News Wall app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.
- Continue Playing Content in Playlist: Check this box to keep this content playing when added to a playlist.

After you've configured the News app to your liking, hit **Save**. Your News app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
