## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- An Instagram **Business** or **Creator** account. Personal accounts can't be used, as this is Meta's policy. Switching is free and only takes a few clicks ([see how](https://help.instagram.com/502981923235522))
- Your Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page ([see how](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050855313))
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))

<a id="InstagramOrInstagramBusiness"></a>

## Instagram or Instagram Business?

OptiSigns has two Instagram apps. Pick the one that matches what you want to show:

- **Instagram** - Shows the posts from your own Instagram profile. You sign in with Instagram directly. This is the app covered in this article.
- **Instagram Business** - Shows the posts from any public profile or a hashtag. You connect through Facebook, using an Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page. See [How to Use the Instagram Business App](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051495193).

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Both apps need an Instagram **Business** or **Creator** account. Personal accounts can't be used, as this is Meta's policy. Switching is free and only takes a few clicks ([see how](https://help.instagram.com/502981923235522)). |

## Creating an Instagram Business App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458047333907)

Search for **Instagram Business** and select the **Instagram Business** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Instagram Business, with the Instagram Business app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458047336083)

Click **Continue with Facebook** and sign in. Facebook requires your Instagram Business account to be connected through Facebook before a third-party app like OptiSigns can use it.

![Instagram Business dialog with the Continue with Facebook button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458056115219)

The Instagram Business app settings will open.

![Instagram Business app form with the Name, Page, Posts Type, Hashtag, Display Mode, Theme, Speed and Font Size fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458047340435)

Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Instagram Business asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Page** - The Facebook Page linked to your Instagram account, if you have more than one. This can only be set when you create the app. To change it later, create a new Instagram Business app.
- **Posts Type** - Choose **Hashtag** or **Profile**. This can only be set when you create the app.
- **Hashtag** or **Profile** - The hashtag (without the #) or the profile's username. For example, enter **nasa** for https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/nasa or https://www.instagram.com/nasa. The link below the field shows the page the app will use, so you can check it on Instagram first.
- **Display Mode** - How your posts are laid out. Choose from **Social Kiosk View**, **Single Post View**, **Social Wall View**, **Timeline View**, **Bricks View**, **Modern View** or **Flip Card View**.
- **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**, or **Custom** to pick your own colors.
- **Speed** - How fast the app moves between posts. Choose **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or **Custom** to set your own speed.
- **Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** to set your own size.

When **Theme** is set to **Custom**, color fields appear so you can set the colors of your wall, such as **Background Color** and **Text Color**. The fields shown depend on your **Display Mode**.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the Background Color and Text Color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458056121747)

When **Speed** is set to **Custom**, drag the slider to choose a speed from 1 to 20.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a slider from 1 to 20](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458056122899)

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing the moderation, post filter, scrolling strip, playlist and QR code options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458056124051)

- **Always show new posts** - On by default, so new posts appear automatically. Turn it off to approve posts before they show, and click the gear icon to review them. Learn more about [moderating your social media feeds](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403015887763).
- **Filter Posts** - Choose **None**, **By Quantity** to show only the latest number of posts you set, or **By Time** to show only posts from the last number of days you set.
- **Only show posts with image** - Turn this on to hide posts without an image.
- **Scrolling Strip Font Size** - If you place the app in a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573) zone set up as a scrolling strip, keep **Default** or choose **Custom** to set the text size.
- **Continue Playing Content in Playlist** - On by default. In a Playlist, the app picks up where it left off each time it comes around, instead of starting again from the first post.
- **Show QR Code** - Turn this on to show a QR code linking to each post.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| For hashtags, the app shows a mix of top posts and recent posts from the last 24 hours. Posts older than 24-48 hours may not appear unless they're among Instagram's top posts for that hashtag. |

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created an Instagram Business app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**. It may take a few seconds for the app to fetch your posts.

## Deploying an Instagram Business App

To push your Instagram Business app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458047346067)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458047348371)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Instagram Business app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Instagram Business asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56458056138643)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Instagram posts will now display on your screen.

Your Instagram Business app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
