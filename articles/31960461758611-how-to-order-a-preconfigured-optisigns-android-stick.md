# How to Order a Preconfigured OptiSigns Android Stick

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/31960461758611-How-to-Order-a-Preconfigured-OptiSigns-Android-Stick
Last updated: 2026-09-10T09:51:37Z

OptiSigns devices are designed for easy, fast deployment. To support it, OptiSigns Android Sticks can be ordered with pre-configuration. You can order your devices directly through our portal, which will pair it to your account and pre-configure the device with screen name, Wi-Fi, and time zone.

**Please note that pre-configuration is available for US customers only.**

| *Note: To learn more about where to buy our devices, please click* [*here*](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/32129032857875)*.* |
| --- |

---

## How to Order Devices:

1. **Login** to your account within [**our portal**](https://app.optisigns.com/app/screenManagement).
2. Either navigate to the [**Screens page**](https://app.optisigns.com/app/screenManagement) and select "**Order Device**" **or** select **"Order Devices"** under your account name.

   ![The Screens page header with the Order Devices button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54796963539219)
3. Select **how many devices** you'd like to order, then select **Next**.

   ![Device price and quantity stepper with the Next: Pre-configure button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54796963540243)
4. You can **name** **your** **Device** (which will have a label on the device), configure it with **Wi-Fi**, and adjust the **time** **zone**. Click **Next**.

   ![Pre-configure your devices with device name, time zone and Wi-Fi columns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54796947289747)
5. Lastly, fill out and review your **payment** and **shipping** information to finish the order. If you have a promo code, that can be input here, as well.

   ![Order Summary at checkout with the promo code field highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/54796963543443)

### Shipping Times

- U.S. customers: Approx. 3-5 days.
- For international customers ordered from Amazon without pre-configuration, estimated shipping time can be found [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/32129032857875).

*Please note that carriers may also experience delays which could extend the estimated delivery timeframes.*
