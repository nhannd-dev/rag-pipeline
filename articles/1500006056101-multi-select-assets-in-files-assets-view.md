# Multi-select assets in Files/Assets view

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500006056101-Multi-select-assets-in-Files-Assets-view
Last updated: 2026-09-10T21:49:44Z

There are a few ways to can select multiple assets at a time:

**1) Click the check mark when hovering your mouse over the asset:**

Hover your mouse over the asset to click the check mark.

![The tick box that appears in the top-left corner of an asset card when you hover over it](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302559075859)

Once multiple items selected, you can use the toolbar to interact with it: Delete, Move, Edit Tags or set them to Live & Expire.

![Three assets selected in Files/Assets, with the action toolbar highlighted at the bottom of the page](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302559076883)

**2) Hold Shift while clicking a second tick box to select every asset between the two**

This is the fastest way to select multiple assets quickly.

**3) Change your preference to Single Click to select:**

In your Personal Settings: <https://app.optisigns.com/app/s/personal-profile>

You can check "Single click to select assets" this will allow you to single click to select the asset instead of opening it. Double Click will open the asset in this case.

![The Personal Profile page with the Single click to select assets tick box highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302559078163)
