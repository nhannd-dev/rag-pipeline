# How to use the TechCrunch News App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040991534-How-to-use-the-TechCrunch-News-App
Last updated: 2026-09-10T09:41:37Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to use the TechCrunch News app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a TechCrunch News Asset](#Creating)

The OptiSigns TechCrunch app allows you to display TechCrunch News on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date Startups, AI, Venture, Security, Crypto, Apps, Fintech, Hardware news and more.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. TechCrunch News can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

## Creating a TechCrunch News Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to **Files/Assets**, then click on **Apps:**

![The Files/Assets page with the Apps button in the left sidebar highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262503562259)

Click **TechCrunch**:

![The Add App dialog searched for TechCrunch, with the TechCrunch app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262503562515)

Enter your TechCrunch Wall App information:

![The TechCrunch Wall settings dialog with the Name field highlighted and a live preview alongside](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262492897171)

- Name: Name of your TechCrunch Wall app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the category or categories you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want. Choose from **Startups, Venture, Security, AI, Crypto, Apps, Fintech, Hardware, Transportation, Media & Entertainment, Social, Mobile, Biotech & Health, Climate, Gadgets, Gaming, Privacy, Space.**
- Theme: Select Light or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![The Speed setting set to Custom, showing the 1-to-20 speed slider it reveals](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262492897939)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the TechCrunch Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![The Advanced link expanded, revealing the Date and Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size settings](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262492899219)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

![The Date and Time setting on Custom, with the custom date format box highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55262503567507)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the TechCrunch News Wall app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the TechCrunch News app to your liking, hit **Save**.

Your TechCrunch app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
