# How to Use the Website Screenshot app

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/10259143299219-How-to-Use-the-Website-Screenshot-app
Last updated: 2026-09-10T09:48:50Z

With the Website Screenshot App, you can capture a screenshot on the URL link and send it to a digital signage screen.

This is particularly useful if you have public website pages running on any browsers that you want to push to your screens, your screens are sometimes offline, or you want to avoid website loading time.

### Let's jump in and get started:

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to Files/Assets, Click on "App", search for and add Website Screenshot to your Account.

![The Add App dialog, searched for Website Screenshot, with the app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55227560038675)

Enter your Website Screenshot app information:

![Website Screenshot settings: Name, URL, Update Interval, Resolution, Full Page and Delay](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55227584971667)

- Name: Name of your Website Screenshot app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- URL: URL link that you want to take Screenshot.
- Update Interval: in hours, how often the Website Screenshot app should take screenshot of your website. Default is 24 hours. If you want to have interval shorter than 1h, it will require Enterprise plan, please contact us at [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com) with some details of your use cases so we can upgrade you.
- Resolution: Select the resolution for your snapshot.
- Full Page: Screenshot of entire website (Note: Infinitely scrolling website will not support)
- Delay: in second. The delay will be applied to allow to take the screenshot.

Click **Save**.

### That's all!

Congratulations! You have created your Website Screenshot app.

You can assign the newly created Website Screenshot app to your screen by going to Screens, click Edit screens and assign the wall to screens that you want.

You can put the walls in a Playlist, Schedule too.
