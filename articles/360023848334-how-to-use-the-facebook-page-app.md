# How to Use the Facebook Page App

Last updated: 2026-09-10T02:49:33Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to set up a Facebook app to display information off Facebook on your digital signs.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)
- [Setting Up a Facebook App](#SettingUp)
  - [Advanced](#Advanced)

With the Facebook app, you're able to display posts and such from public-facing Facebook pages you have ownership access over.

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYouNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Free Plan or higher**](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- A Facebook account
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653-What-hardware-and-devices-are-supported) - if on Free plan, one of [these devices](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/33940834613139-What-Do-I-Get-With-an-OptiSigns-Free-Plan#Devices)
- A screen, [set up and paired with OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891-OptiSigns-Getting-Started-Guide)

<a id="SettingUp"></a>

## Setting Up a Facebook App

On the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, then click **Apps** in the left sidebar.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467950483)

Select **Facebook**:

![Add App dialog with Facebook in the search box and the Facebook app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467957523)

Login to Facebook. If this is the first time registering it with OptiSigns, you will need to hit **Connect Facebook Pages** then log in.

![Facebook Page - Wall panel showing Login with Facebook and Connect Facebook Pages](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467958419)

The Facebook App allows you to register numerous Facebook accounts. If you have already registered your account, you can choose it with the **Select** option.

After logging on with Facebook in the Facebook popup window, select the Pages you want OptiSigns to access data to create your Wall.

Click **Next**, then Click **Done**.

Whether you connect a new Facebook page or choose it from an existing one, you'll see the below screen:

![Facebook wall settings: Name, Page, Display Mode, Theme, Speed and Font Size](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248514541331)

After that, you can enter information for your wall.

- **Name**: Name of your Facebook Wall, this is the name of the wall in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- **Page**: Page you want to use (if you have more than 1).

  | **IMPORTANT** |
  | --- |
  | A Facebook asset can only access a single page, and this can only be changed during the creation of the asset. In order to change this, you will need to delete this asset and create a new one.  If you wish to display multiple Facebook pages, you will need to create individual assets for each one, then use them in a [Playlist](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28295104605843-How-to-Create-Use-Playlists). |
- **Display Mode**: Choose how you want your Facebook page to display. There are numerous options here - play around with them, and they should automatically populate the Preview area on the right. Choose the one which suits you best!
- **Theme**: Change between Light and Dark themes.
- **Speed**: This determines how quickly your Facebook posts cycle. Choose between **Slow**, **Medium**, **Fast**, and **Custom**. When Custom is chosen, you will see the following slider:

  ![Speed set to Custom, showing the speed slider from 1 to 20 with Slow, Medium, Fast](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467961107)
- **Font Size**: Set the font size. Default is 20, but this can be set to **Custom** and changed to whatever you wish.
- **Landscape / Portrait:** Choose the orientation for the Preview.

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click the **Advanced** option to show more settings:

![Advanced section expanded showing Moderation, Filter Type and Show QR Code](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467963155)

- **Moderation:** Clicking Moderation opens up a popup:

![Moderation popup listing a Facebook post with hide and delete buttons](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248514549267)

This screen lists the posts OptiSigns has pulled from your Page and lets you control which of them reach your screens. Anything you hide or delete here applies to this Facebook asset only — your Facebook Page itself is not changed.

- **Search:** Find a post by its text. Type a word or phrase and click the magnifier button — the list then shows only the posts that match.
- **Newest / Oldest:** Choose the order the posts are listed in. **Newest** is the default.
- **Refresh Data:** Fetch the latest posts from your Page. Use this if a post you just published has not appeared yet — it can take a few moments for the app to pick it up.
- **Hide:** The eye-with-a-slash button at the end of a post stops that post appearing on your screens, but keeps it in this list so you can bring it back later.
- **Delete:** The red trash button removes the post from this wall. You are asked to confirm first.
- **First / Previous / Next / Last:** Page through the list when your Page has more posts than fit on one screen.
- **Close:** Return to the app settings.

![Close-up of a post row with the hide and delete buttons at its right-hand end](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248514551443)

Back in **Advanced**, the remaining settings are:

- **Always show new posts:** When checked, this will ensure you are only showing new posts
- **Filter Type:** Choose to Filter by Quantity or by Time. If one of these is selected, you can choose how many posts to show, or across what timeframe to show posts.
- **Only show posts with image:** When selected, will only show posts that have images. If you want to display text-only Facebook posts, leave this unchecked.
- **Scrolling Strip Font Size:** Set the font size of the scrolling strip. Default is 20, but this can be set to **Custom** and changed to whatever you wish.
- **Continue Playing Content in Playlist:** When checked, if the Facebook wall is placed in a Playlist, it will continue displaying posts from where it left off. Otherwise, it will always start with the first post in your sequence.
- **Show QR Code**: When checked, allows you to add a QR code. You can choose for the QR code to go to the Facebook page, or to any other page by choosing **Custom**.

  ![Show QR Code checked, with QR Code set to Custom and a QR Code URL field](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467979795)

Finally, hit **Save**. You've created a Facebook asset.

## Deploying a Facebook App

You can deploy your new Facebook app as an individual asset, or as part of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573-How-to-Create-and-Use-the-Split-Screen-App).

To get your new Facebook asset to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** on the screen you want to assign it to.

![Screens list with a screen row highlighted and its Edit button magnified below](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467984403)

This brings up the **Edit Screen** dialog:

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248514561555)

Here, select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change**.

Then, select your created Facebook Asset:

![Select Asset dialog with the Facebook Page asset highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55248467990419)

Now hit **Save**. Your Facebook asset will now display on screen.

You can also deploy it as part of a split screen, allowing you to show other assets at the same time. See how in our [Split Screen app article.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573-How-to-Create-and-Use-the-Split-Screen-App)

In order to display multiple Facebook pages, you can create individual page assets, then place them in a [Playlist](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28295104605843-How-to-Create-Use-Playlists). The asset or playlist can also be placed in a [Schedule](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016981853-Creating-and-Using-Schedules-with-OptiSigns).
