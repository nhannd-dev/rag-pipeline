# Sharing Playlists and Folders with External Users

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/21708242980755-Sharing-Playlists-and-Folders-with-External-Users
Last updated: 2026-09-11T17:02:25Z

Making it effortless to share playlists and folders with external users has become a must. Exclusive to our Pro Plus and higher plans, this feature opens the door to a wide array of content contributions, encompassing images, videos, and documents—essential for enhancing collaboration.

Furthermore, there are several compelling use cases for this feature:

- Media advertising companies can collaborate with partner locations such as doctor's offices, streamlining content creation efforts without the need for additional sign-ups or logins.
- Major retail brands aspire to enable retail locations to enrich shared playlists with localized content.
- Educational institutions and offices seek a streamlined solution that empowers local administrators to efficiently contribute their own content.

In this article, we'll guide you on how to seamlessly share your playlist and/or folder with external users, making collaboration a breeze.

---

## Ready to get started? Then, let's jump in:

### How to Share Your Playlist

Go to Playlists. Create a new playlist to share or share an existing playlist.

Check the box to the right of the selected playlist.

Then click 'Share Link':

![OptiSigns Playlists panel with one playlist ticked; the Share Link icon in the selection toolbar is highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958886931)

In the pop-up, click on 'Create Share Link':

![Share Link dialog for a playlist reading 'No share link exists for this item', with Create Share Link highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967025683)

Enter the email addresses of the external users you'd like to share it with and hit 'Enter'.

![Share Link dialog showing General Access set to Restricted above the Share via Email address field.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958892307)

**Share Playlist Options:**

![Share via Email block: one address added, role Viewer, Notify Email ticked, BCC Myself, Message box and Share.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967028243)

- **General Access** - Decide how you would like your external user to collaborate with your playlist
  - **Editor** - Allows users to add, delete, and modify content within the shared playlist
  - **Viewer** - Allows users to view the contents within the shared playlist
- **Share via Email** -
  - **Notify Email** - Allows you to send email notifications to recipients, informing them of your intention to share a playlist and providing convenient access links directly within their email.
  - **BCC Myself** - You can BCC yourself if would like to receive a copy of the email in your own inbox without the recipient knowing.
- **Message** - You can include a personalized message to accompany the shared content, providing additional context, instructions, or any relevant information for the recipients.

**General Access Options:**

![General Access dropdown open showing Restricted (selected), Your Account Members and Anyone with the Link.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967030291)

- **Restricted**: Only specific individuals or a defined group of people are granted access to the file or folder.
- **Your Account Members**: Limited to members of your OptiSigns account.
- **Anyone with the Link:** Anyone who possesses the unique URL/link can access the content without requiring specific account authentication.

**Advanced Options**

![Advanced section expanded, highlighting the Enable Access Password and Enable Expiration toggles.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967031699)

- **Enable Access Password** - Users must enter the password to open and view the content; Enhances security by adding an additional layer of authentication.
  - When enabled, you can either type in your custom password or click on 'Create Password' for the system to generate a password.

    ![Enable Access Password switched on, showing the password field with Create Password and Reveal Password.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967035539)
  - Click on 'Update'
    - Password is automatically hidden when the pop-up is closed. To reveal the password, click on 'Reveal Password'

![Enable Access Password switched on, showing the password field with Create Password and Reveal Password.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967035539)

- **Enable Expiration** - Access to the content automatically expires after the specified date and time; Controls access duration, useful for time-sensitive or temporary sharing.
  - When enabled, you can choose the date and time you would like the link to expire. Click 'Apply'

![Enable Expiration switched on, showing the expiration date and time fields.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958900627)

Click on 'Share' when ready. This requires at least one email address to be added.

![Share via Email block with the Share button highlighted below the optional Message box.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967042963)

You can also share the link manually without sending email notifications by clicking on 'Copy Link'. This will automatically copy the link to clipboard.

![Share Link dialog footer with Copy Link highlighted, beside the Unshare and Cancel buttons.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958903187)

If you no longer wish to share the playlist, you can click on 'Unshare' to retract or revoke access to the playlist and its content.

![Share Link dialog footer with the red Unshare button highlighted, beside Copy Link and Cancel.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958905747)

You have the flexibility to manage the shared playlist effortlessly, allowing you to seamlessly add new collaborators, adjust their permissions, or remove access whenever needed.

### How to Share Your Folder

Sharing your folder is a very similar process to sharing your playlist, making it an intuitive and straightforward experience.

Go to Files/Assets and select the folder you wish to share.

On the top right hand corner, click on the 3 dots in the folder card. Only folders have Share Link - assets do not.

Then click 'Share Link':

![Folder actions menu on a Files/Assets folder card with the Share Link item highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328967047699)

In the pop-up, click on 'Create Share Link'

![Share Link dialog for a folder reading 'No share link exists for this item', with Create Share Link highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958908819)

Enter the email addresses of the external users you'd like to share it with and hit 'Enter'.

![Folder Share Link dialog: General Access set to Anyone with the Link with Editor role, and the email field.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55328958910995)

**NOTE:** The features, functionality, and settings for sharing, general access, and advanced options remain consistent with those of sharing a playlist.
