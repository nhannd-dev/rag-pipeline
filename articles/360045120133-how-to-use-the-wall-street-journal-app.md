# How to Use the Wall Street Journal App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045120133-How-to-Use-the-Wall-Street-Journal-App
Last updated: 2026-09-10T20:57:02Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to use The Wall Street Journal app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a The Wall Street Journal Asset](#Creating)

The OptiSigns Wall Street Journal app allows you to display The Wall Street Journal on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date Opinion pieces, World News stories, US Business pieces, Market news, Technology, and Lifestyle pieces.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. The Wall Street Journal can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

![The Wall Street Journal app on a screen: an Opinion headline, photo, date and a QR code to open the article.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/45517575893779)

## Creating a The Wall Street Journal Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to **Files/Assets**, Click on "Apps" and add **The Wall Street Journal** on your Account.

![The Files/Assets page in the OptiSigns portal, with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275357504915)

Click **The Wall Street Journal**:

![The Add App dialog, searched for Wall Street, with The Wall Street Journal app card highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275341530899)

Enter your Wall Street Journal App information:

![The Wall Street Journal Wall settings filled in, with a live preview and the Save button highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275341614483)

- Name: Name of your Wall Street Journal app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the region and category that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want.
- Theme: Select Light or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, highlighting the dropdown and the 1-to-20 slider marked Slow, Medium and Fast.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275324517267)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the Wall Street Journal app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![The Advanced toggle highlighted, revealing the Date & Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size options.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275335950867)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date & Time set to Custom, highlighting the custom format field showing MMMM DD, YYYY hh:mm A.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55275341821459)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the Wall Street Journal app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the Wall Street Journal app to your liking, hit **Save**. Your Wall Street Journal app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
