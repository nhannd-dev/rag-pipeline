# Changing screen's orientation (portrait & landscape)

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016833514-Changing-screen-s-orientation-portrait-landscape
Last updated: 2026-09-17T04:18:56Z

OptiSigns supports rotation of your screens:

- Landscape (0 Degree)
- Rotate 90 Degrees
- Rotate 180 Degrees
- Rotate 270 Degrees

Of course, for best display experience, please ensure you have content that would fit the portrait modes. Our apps are designed to fit portrait mode as well.

You can do so by updating the screen's setting on the portal (<https://app.optisigns.com/>):

![Edit Screen dialog with the Orientation dropdown open: Landscape, Rotate 90, 180 and 270 degrees](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55497527594259)

Or on the device's side menu:

![OptiSigns player side menu on the device, with the Rotation row set to Landscape](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360025424053)

![Rotation dialog on the device player: Landscape and Rotate 90, 180, 270 degrees, Landscape selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360025424473)
