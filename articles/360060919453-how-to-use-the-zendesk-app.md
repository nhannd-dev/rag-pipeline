# How to Use the Zendesk App

Last updated: 2026-10-14T18:07:38Z

### In this article, we'll show how to display your Zendesk dashboards and views on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Creating a Zendesk App](#CreatingAZendeskApp)
- [Signing In to Zendesk](#SigningInToZendesk)
- [Deploying a Zendesk App](#DeployingAZendeskApp)

Zendesk is a customer service platform for managing support tickets, chat and help centers. With the Zendesk app, it's possible to display your Zendesk dashboards and views on your digital signs, so your support team can keep an eye on queues and performance without opening a laptop.

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYoullNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- A Zendesk account, with access to the dashboard or view you want to display
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))
- A keyboard and mouse you can connect to the device, for the first sign-in

<a id="CreatingAZendeskApp"></a>

## Creating a Zendesk App

First, open the Zendesk dashboard or view you want to display, such as a dashboard in Zendesk Explore. Copy its address from your browser's address bar.

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417385314323)

Search for **Zendesk** and select the **Zendesk** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Zendesk, with the Zendesk app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417385316115)

The Zendesk app settings will open. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Zendesk asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **URL** - The Zendesk address you copied.
- **Update Interval** - How often the app checks Zendesk for updates, measured in seconds. The default is 600 (every 10 minutes). Set it to 0 to check only when the app first loads.

![Zendesk app form with Name, URL and Update Interval filled in, and an arrow on Save](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417385318419)

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Zendesk app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

![The saved Zendesk app asset in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417385319059)

<a id="SigningInToZendesk"></a>

## Signing In to Zendesk

Zendesk dashboards and views are private, so the screen needs to sign in to Zendesk to show them. The Zendesk app doesn't store a login in OptiSigns. Instead, you sign in once on the device itself.

The first time the app plays on a device, the Zendesk sign-in page will display. Connect a keyboard and mouse to the device, then enter the username and password of the Zendesk account the screen should use. After that, the device stays signed in.

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| On an Amazon Fire TV Stick, you can type with the [Fire TV app on your phone](https://www.lifewire.com/use-phone-as-remote-control-for-amazon-fire-tv-stick-4571277) or a Bluetooth keyboard instead. |

For more on displaying pages that require a login, see [How to display content inside websites that require login](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037391854-How-to-display-content-inside-websites-that-require-login-).

<a id="DeployingAZendeskApp"></a>

## Deploying a Zendesk App

To push your Zendesk app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens page with an arrow on a screen's Edit button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417385320083)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and an arrow on Change](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417393582483)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Zendesk app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Zendesk app asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56417393584531)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Zendesk dashboard will now display on your screen.

Your Zendesk app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
