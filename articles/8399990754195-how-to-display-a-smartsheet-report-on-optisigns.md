# How to Display a Smartsheet Report on OptiSigns

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/8399990754195-How-to-Display-a-Smartsheet-Report-on-OptiSigns
Last updated: 2026-10-12T18:33:37Z

Smartsheet is a popular SaaS platform for project & work management.

With OptiSigns, you can easily display your Smartsheet reports and dashboards on your screens.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| This app is for displaying shared Smartsheet reports requiring authentication. If your report is published on Smartsheet, it will have a published HTML URL. You can use this URL with our Website or Advanced Website app to display the report. |

![A Smartsheet project sheet in Gantt view, showing tasks with status, health and start dates](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/8400032137875)

## Let's jump in and get started:

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891-OptiSigns-Getting-Started-Guide).

On the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Asset > Apps:**

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Files/Assets tab and the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56342011665299)

Find and click on Smartsheet:

![Add App dialog with Smartsheet typed in the search box and the Smartsheet app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56342011667475)

Next, enter details for your Smartsheet report:

![Smartsheet app dialog with Name, URL and Update Interval filled and Sign in with Credential turned on](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56342011668499)

- Name: Name of your asset. This is just to name the asset within OptiSigns. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- URL: the URL of your Smartsheet report.
- Update Interval: How often to check Smartsheet for updates to the report. The default is every 10 minutes.
- Toggle **Sign In with Credential** to enter:
  - Master Password: your password is encrypted with OptiSigns, but you can add another layer of encryption with your Master Password. This way even OptiSigns cannot decrypt your password. You will need to enter this Master Password on each device to decrypt at device level. Learn more about our zero-knowledge encryption methodology [here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500012522362-How-to-use-Web-Scripting-App).
  - Username/Email: your Smartsheet account username or email.
  - Password: your Smartsheet account password

Click **Save**.

To get the Smartsheet report URL, you can go to your Smartsheet report and click "Share". Then you can get the URL from the Sheet Link section.

![A Smartsheet sheet with the Share button at the top right highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/8400222153875)

![Smartsheet Sheet Sharing dialog with the Sheet Link URL highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/8400310710931)

### That's all!

Congratulations, you have created a Smartsheet asset, now you can assign it to your screens, playlist, schedule like any other assets.
