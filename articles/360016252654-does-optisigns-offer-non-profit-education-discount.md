# Does OptiSigns offer Non-profit/Education discount?

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016252654-Does-OptiSigns-offer-Non-profit-Education-discount
Last updated: 2026-09-29T17:04:04Z

Yes, we are supportive of nonprofit organizations and organizations in the education field.

For eligible nonprofit or education organizations, we offer a flat 10% discount on subscriptions. Please note that this discount applies to subscription fees only and does not apply to hardware purchases.

To request the discount, please email us at [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com) with your organization’s website and documentation that helps us verify your nonprofit, education, or tax-exempt status. A 501(c)(3) document is commonly used, but we understand that not every organization has this specific document. Other official documentation showing your organization’s nonprofit, education, or tax-exempt status may also be accepted.

Once your eligibility is verified, we will apply the discount to your account going forward.

If you have any additional questions or feedback about OptiSigns, please feel free to contact our support team at [support@optisigns.com](mailto:support@optisigns.com).
