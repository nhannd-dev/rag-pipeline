# How to use the CNN Wall App

Last updated: 2026-09-10T09:41:46Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to use the CNN News app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a CNN News Asset](#Creating)

The OptiSigns CNN app allows you to display CNN News on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date US, Politics, Business, Sport, Entertainment, Football, Health, World, Technology, Europe, Travel, and Most Recent news.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. CNN News can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

## Creating a CNN News Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: [https://app.optisigns.com/](http://app.optisigns.com/)

Go to **Files/Assets**, Click on "Apps" and add **CNN** on your Account.

![The Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252921439379)

Click **CNN**:

![The Add App dialog with CNN searched and the CNN app tile highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252930430483)

Enter your CNN Wall App information:

![The CNN Wall form: Name, Category checkboxes, Theme, Speed and Font Size, with a live Preview.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252921442707)

- Name: Name of your CNN Wall app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the region and category that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want. You'll need to select at least one.
- Theme: Select Light (default CNN red) or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, revealing a 1-20 slider marked Slow, Medium and Fast.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252921444499)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the CNN Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![Advanced expanded, showing the Date and Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size fields.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252921446675)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date and Time set to Custom, with an unlabelled format field beside it.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55252921448339)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the CNN News Wall app to a Split Screen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the CNN News app to your liking, hit **Save**. Your CNN app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
