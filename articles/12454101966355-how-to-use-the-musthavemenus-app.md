# How to Use the MustHaveMenus App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/12454101966355-How-to-Use-the-MustHaveMenus-App
Last updated: 2026-10-13T20:53:34Z

### In this article, we'll show how to display your MustHaveMenus menu designs on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Creating a MustHaveMenus App](#CreatingAMustHaveMenusApp)
- [Deploying a MustHaveMenus App](#DeployingAMustHaveMenusApp)

[MustHaveMenus](https://www.musthavemenus.com/) makes it easy to design professional menus for restaurants, cafes and bars. With the MustHaveMenus app, it's possible to put those menu designs on your digital signs as a digital menu board, and have the screen pick up your changes whenever you edit the design.

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYoullNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- A MustHaveMenus account on a plan that lets you share links to your designs (MustHaveMenus **Pro**)
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| MustHaveMenus now requires a paid **Pro** plan to share links to your designs. If you don't have one, you can use OptiSigns' free [menu board templates](https://canvas.optisigns.com/) and [POS integrations instead (requires OptiSigns Pro Plus plan or above)](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/31860170199955-Integrating-Point-of-Sale-POS-Systems-to-Build-Digital-Menu-Boards-with-OptiSync). |

## Creating a MustHaveMenus App

First, sign in to **musthavemenus.com** and open the menu design you want to display. Click **Share**, select **Share Link**, then hit **Copy**.

![MustHaveMenus design editor with Share and Share Link highlighted and an arrow pointing to Copy](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386438653459)

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![Files/Assets page in OptiSigns with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386454968723)

Search for **MustHaveMenus** and select the **MustHaveMenus** app.

![Add App dialog searched for MustHaveMenus, with the MustHaveMenus app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386438657811)

The MustHaveMenus app settings will open. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your MustHaveMenus asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **URL** - The share link you copied from MustHaveMenus.
- **Update Interval** - How often the app checks MustHaveMenus for changes to your design, measured in seconds. The default is 600 (every 10 minutes). Set it to 0 to check only when the app first loads.
- **Menu Page** - The page of your design to show, as a number. The default is 1.

![MustHaveMenus app form with Name, URL, Update Interval and Menu Page filled in, and an arrow pointing to Save](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386454972435)

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| Each MustHaveMenus app shows one page. To show a design with several pages, create one MustHaveMenus app per page, each with a different **Menu Page**, and put them in a Playlist or a Split Screen. |

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a MustHaveMenus app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

![New MustHaveMenus asset shown in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386454973331)

## Deploying a MustHaveMenus App

To push your MustHaveMenus app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386438659603)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and an arrow pointing to Change](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386438660627)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your MustHaveMenus app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the MustHaveMenus asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56386438664211)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your menu will now display on your screen.

Your MustHaveMenus app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
