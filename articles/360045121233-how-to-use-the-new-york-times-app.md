# How to Use the New York Times app

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045121233-How-to-Use-the-New-York-Times-app
Last updated: 2026-09-10T21:43:23Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to use the New York Times app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040991534#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a The New York Times News Asset](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040991534#Creating)

The OptiSigns New York Times app allows you to display news from The New York Times on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date Top Stories, World news, Business, Sports, Economy, Culture, Lifestyle news and more.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. The New York Times News can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

## Creating a The New York Times News Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to **Files/Assets**, then click **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Files/Assets tab and the Apps button in the left panel highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302330309651)

Click **The New York Times**:

![Add App dialog searched for New York Times, with The New York Times app card highlighted next to RSS.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302335985299)

Enter your The New York Times Wall App information:

![The New York Times Wall setup form: Name field and the Category checkbox list, with a live Preview panel.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302330316179)

- Name: Name of your The New York Times Wall app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the region and category that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want.

![Theme, Speed and Font Size dropdowns in the New York Times Wall form, all set to their defaults.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302335992979)

- Theme: Select Light or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a 1-to-20 slider marked Slow, Medium and Fast with the handle at 10.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302335995411)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the The New York Times Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing the Date & Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size dropdowns.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302330320915)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date & Time set to Custom, with a format field showing the placeholder MMMM DD, YYYY hh:mm A.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55302330322451)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the The New York Times News Wall app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the New York Times News app to your liking, hit **Save**.

Your New York Times app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
