# How to Change Engage/Kiosk Style Preferences

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28115946505107-How-to-Change-Engage-Kiosk-Style-Preferences
Last updated: 2026-09-10T21:01:31Z

When you change your Engage/Kiosk Styles Preferences, it will adjust the default options set for your Kiosk Lite and Kiosk Designer Pro.

While you can modify the settings individually for specific apps, setting a default will help maintain consistency, ensuring your preferences remain unchanged unless manually adjusted.

You can adjust these settings by:

- First, click your profile name in the top right corner of your OptiSigns webpage.
- Then, click the "**Preferences**" tab in the drop-down menu.

![OptiSigns user menu open with Preferences highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55300647478931)

- You will then be directed to the Preferences page. To access the Engage/Kiosk Styles settings, scroll down to the bottom of the page.

![Engage/Kiosk Styles preferences card with Save Changes highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55300647483667)

**Engage/Kiosk Styles:**

- **Default Icon Position**: Adjust the placement of icons within the bar to optimize visibility and access: none, top left, top center, top right, bottom left, bottom center, bottom right
- **Default Icon Style:** Select from a variety of icon designs to match your kiosk's aesthetic: Default, Label, Solid, or Round
- **Default Icon Color**: Customize the color of your icons to complement your brand or theme.
- **Default Icon Size**: Scale the icons to the ideal size for easy recognition and usability. Choose from Default, Small, Medium, Large, or Custom Sizing.
- **Default Background Color:** You can customize the color for the navigation bar background to align with your design scheme.
- **Default Opacity:** Adjust the transparency of the navigation bar for subtle or bold visibility.

Click **Save Changes**.

---

## That's all!

You can change these default settings at any time or adjust the settings manually within the kiosk apps.
