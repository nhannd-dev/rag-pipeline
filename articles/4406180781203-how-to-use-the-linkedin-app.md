## Before You Begin: Set Up Your Company Page

The LinkedIn app shows posts from a LinkedIn **Company Page**, not a personal profile.

You'll need to [create a company page](https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/linkedin-pages) if you don't have one.

Your LinkedIn account will also need to have admin access. If you don't have it, you'll need to [request admin access](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/17481/request-admin-access-to-a-linkedin-page?lang=en). Ask a current admin of the Company Page to approve your request. In LinkedIn, they go to **Admin tools** > **Settings** > **Manage admins**, choose a role for you, and click **Add admin**.

![LinkedIn Admin tools menu with Manage admins under Settings](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/4406192461715)

![LinkedIn role dropdown with the Add admin button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/4406180621971)

Once you're an admin, you're ready to connect LinkedIn to OptiSigns.

## Creating a LinkedIn App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264038547)

Search for **LinkedIn** and select the **LinkedIn** app.

![Add App dialog searched for LinkedIn, with the LinkedIn app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463231211411)

Click **Sign in with LinkedIn** and sign in with your LinkedIn account.

![LinkedIn app window with the Sign in with LinkedIn button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264040851)

Once you're signed in, the LinkedIn app settings will open.

![LinkedIn app form showing the Name, Company, Display Mode, Theme, Speed and Font Size fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463231215379)

Fill them in:

- **Name** - The name of your LinkedIn asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Company** - The Company Page whose posts you want to show, if you're an admin of more than one.

| **IMPORTANT** |
| --- |
| You can only set the **Company** when you create the app. To show a different Company Page later, create a new LinkedIn app. |

- **Display Mode** - How your posts are laid out. Choose from **Social Kiosk View**, **Single Post View**, **Social Wall View**, **Timeline View**, **Bricks View**, **Modern View** or **Flip Card View**.
- **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**, or **Custom** to pick your own colors.
- **Speed** - How fast the app moves between posts. Choose **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or **Custom** to set your own speed.
- **Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** to set your own size.

When **Theme** is set to **Custom**, color fields appear so you can set the colors of your wall, such as **Background Color** and **Text Color**. The fields shown depend on your **Display Mode**.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the Background Color and Text Color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264041363)

When **Speed** is set to **Custom**, drag the slider to choose a speed from 1 to 20.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a slider from 1 to 20](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264042003)

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing the moderation, post filter, scrolling strip and playlist options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463231217427)

- **Always show new posts** - On by default, so every new post shows up automatically with no review needed. Turn it off to approve posts before they appear. Click the gear icon next to it to open **Moderation**, where you can show or hide individual posts. You can read more about [content moderation here](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403015887763).
- **Filter Posts** - Choose **None**, **By Quantity** to show only the latest number of posts you set, or **By Time** to show only posts from the last number of days you set.
- **Only show posts with image** - Turn this on to hide posts without an image.
- **Scrolling Strip Font Size** - If you place the app in a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573) zone set up as a scrolling strip, keep **Default** or choose **Custom** to set the text size.
- **Continue Playing Content in Playlist** - On by default. In a Playlist, the app picks up where it left off each time it comes around, instead of starting again from the first post.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a LinkedIn app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**. It may take a few seconds for the app to fetch your posts.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Video playback isn't supported in the LinkedIn app. If you want to show LinkedIn videos, check out the [Taggbox Display app](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009343862). |

## Deploying a LinkedIn App

To push your LinkedIn app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264051219)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463264053907)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your LinkedIn app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the LinkedIn asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56463231222675)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your LinkedIn posts will now display on your screen.

Your LinkedIn app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
