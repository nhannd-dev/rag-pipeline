# How to Use the YouTube Playlist App with OptiSigns

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059386254-How-to-Use-the-YouTube-Playlist-App-with-OptiSigns
Last updated: 2026-09-14T18:47:59Z

Putting YouTube playlist on your digital signs screens is easy with OptiSigns.
You just need to copy the YouTube Playlist Link and paste in the YouTube Playlist app in OptiSigns.

This article will guide you through how to create and use a YouTube Playlist App.

## Let's jump in and get started:

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: [https://app.optisigns.com/](http://app.optisigns.com/)

Go to **Files/Assets**, then click on **Apps:**

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55402483468563)

Click **YouTube Playlist** app:

![Add App dialog searched for Youtube Playlist, with the YouTube Playlist app tile highlighted.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55402502821395)

Enter your YouTube Playlist information:

![YouTube Playlist form: Name and URL fields, Caption and Shuffle toggles, Close and Save buttons.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55402502823443)

- Name: Give your YouTube Playlist a name. This is for displaying in your asset list only. The app will try to get the video's title and use as a name, but you can change it to something else.
- URL: Paste the link to your YouTube playlist here.
- Caption: Toggle Captions on or off. If the YouTube video has closed caption, the app will show it. Note: closed caption must be created by the YouTube video author. Auto-generated captions may not work.
- Shuffle: Toggle Shuffle on or off. This will randomize the videos within the Playlist.

Click **Save**.

---

### That's all! Congratulations!

You have added a YouTube playlist video.

You can assign the newly created YouTube playlist asset to your screen by going to Screens, click Edit screens and assign the app to screens that you want.

You can put the YouTube playlist to a Playlist or a Schedule too.
