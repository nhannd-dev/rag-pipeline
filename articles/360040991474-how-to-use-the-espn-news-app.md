### In this article, we'll explain how to use the ESPN News app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a ESPN News Asset](#Creating)

The OptiSigns ESPN app allows you to display ESPN News on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date Top Headlines, NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, College Sports, and more.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. ESPN News can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

## Creating a ESPN News Asset

Log in to our portal: [https://app.optisigns.com/](http://app.optisigns.com/).

Then go to **Files/Assets**, Click on **Apps** and add **ESPN** on your Account.

![Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left sidebar](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264464642451)

Click **ESPN**:

![Add App dialog with the ESPN app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264472855571)

Enter your ESPN Wall App information:

![ESPN Wall settings: Name, Category, Theme, Speed, Font Size, and a live preview](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264472856083)

- Name: Name of your ESPN Wall app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the category or categories that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want.
- Theme: Select Light (default ESPN red) or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, showing the 1-to-20 speed slider](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264464645139)

- Font Size: Set the font size for the ESPN Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing Date and Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264472860819)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date and Time set to Custom, with the custom format box beside it](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55264464650131)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the ESPN News Wall app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the ESPN News app to your liking, hit **Save**.

Your ESPN app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
