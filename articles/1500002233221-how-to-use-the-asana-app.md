# How to Use the Asana App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500002233221-How-to-Use-the-Asana-App
Last updated: 2026-10-13T16:34:14Z

### In this article, we'll show how to display your Asana projects on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Creating an Asana App](#CreatingAnAsanaApp)
- [Deploying an Asana App](#DeployingAnAsanaApp)

With the Asana app, it's possible to display your team's Asana projects on your digital signs. Keep tasks, deadlines and project progress in front of everyone, in the office, on the shop floor or in the break room, and let the screen refresh itself as the project changes.

Let's get started.

---

<a id="WhatYoullNeed"></a>

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - [**Standard Plan** or higher](https://www.optisigns.com/pricing)
- An Asana account, with access to the project you want to display
- An [OptiSigns-enabled device](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360021855653)
- A screen, set up and paired with OptiSigns ([see our Getting Started guide](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/18823504383891))

<a id="CreatingAnAsanaApp"></a>

## Creating an Asana App

First, open the Asana project you want to display at **app.asana.com**. Copy the full address of the project from your browser's address bar.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| The OptiSigns Asana app only accepts an **app.asana.com** link that ends in a number. If the address you paste ends in a word such as **/list** or **/board**, the app shows **Please enter a valid Asana's URL** and you won't be able to save. |

Next, go to the OptiSigns portal. Go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted in the left panel](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375089191571)

Search for **Asana** and select the **Asana** app.

![Add App dialog with Asana typed in the search box and the Asana app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375089193491)

The Asana app settings will open. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Asana asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **URL** - The Asana project address you copied in the first step.
- **Update Interval** - How often the app checks Asana for updates, measured in seconds. The default is 600 (every 10 minutes). Set it to 0 to check only when the app first loads.

![Asana app settings with Name, URL and Update Interval filled in and the Save button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375078298899)

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created an Asana app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

![Files/Assets list showing the new Team Project Board Asana asset](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375078300307)

| **IMPORTANT** |
| --- |
| If your Asana project is private, the screen will show the Asana sign-in page the first time it plays. Sign in once on the device and choose to remember your login. On devices without a keyboard, such as a Fire TV Stick, connect a Bluetooth keyboard or mouse to sign in. For more detail, see [How to display content inside websites that require login](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037391854). |

## Deploying an Asana App

To push your Asana app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted for a screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375089198227)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen panel with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375078304147)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Asana app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset window with the Team Project Board Asana asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56375089205011)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Asana project will now display on your screen.

Your Asana app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
