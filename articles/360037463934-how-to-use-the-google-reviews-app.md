With the Google Reviews app, it's possible to show your business's Google reviews on your screens, alongside a map and a QR code. Show off what your customers are saying, and make it easy for new visitors to scan the code and leave a review of their own.

![Google Reviews App example](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360049356974)

Let's get started.

## Creating a Google Reviews App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341569811)

Search for **Google Reviews** and select the **Google Reviews** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Google Reviews, with the Google Reviews app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341573523)

Choose how you'll find your reviews:

- **Enter Public Place** - Search for any business or place on Google Maps. Google only shares up to 5 recent reviews this way.
- **Sign in with Google** - Sign in with the Google account that manages your business's Google Business Profile to show your own place's reviews. See [Using Sign in with Google](#UsingSignInWithGoogle) below.

For this example, we'll select **Enter Public Place**.

![Google Reviews dialog with the Enter Public Place button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326934035)

Give your wall a **Name**, then start typing your business into **Place**. Select your business from the suggestions.

![Place field searched for Space Center Houston, with the first suggestion highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326935827)

The **Google Reviews** app settings will appear, with a map of the place you chose.

![Google Reviews app form with the Name and Place filled in and a map of the place](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326936851)

Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Google Reviews asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Place** - The business or place whose reviews you want to show.
- **Rating** - Only show reviews with a certain star rating. Choose from **Any**, **5**, **4+**, **3+** or **3 or less**.
- **Language** - The language for the wall's labels.
- **Display Mode** - The layout used for the wall. Choose from **Review**, **Box**, **Carousel** or **Sidebar**. See [Display Modes](#DisplayModes) below.
- **Show Map** - Turn this on to show a map of your place on the wall.
- **Show nearby places** - Turn this on to show nearby businesses and points of interest on the map. See [Show Nearby Places](#ShowNearbyPlaces) below.

<a id="DisplayModes"></a>

### Display Modes

**Display Mode** changes how your reviews are laid out on the screen.

- **Review** - One review at a time, shown next to the map.

![Google Reviews wall in the Review display mode, showing one review beside the map](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326937747)

- **Box** - A grid of reviews shown next to the map.

![Google Reviews wall in the Box display mode, showing a grid of six reviews beside the map](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341580947)

- **Carousel** - A row of reviews shown under the map and business details.

![Google Reviews wall in the Carousel display mode, showing three reviews in a row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326940051)

- **Sidebar** - Your business details and map on the left, with a column of reviews on the right.

![Google Reviews wall in the Sidebar display mode, showing a column of reviews on the right](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326942995)

<a id="ShowNearbyPlaces"></a>

### Show Nearby Places

If you're a hotel, you may want to turn on **Show nearby places** so viewers can see what's around you. If you're a restaurant and don't want to show competitors in your area, leave it off.

Here's how it looks with **Show nearby places** turned on. Notice the businesses and points of interest on the map:

![Google Reviews wall with Show nearby places on, with arrows pointing to nearby businesses on the map](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360049148194)

And here it is turned off, with no other businesses shown on the map:

![Google Reviews wall with Show nearby places off, showing no other businesses on the map](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360049148174)

<a id="Content"></a>

### Content

Click **Content** to choose what appears on the wall:

![Content section expanded, showing Show Review Date, Show QR Code, QR Code and Leave a Review Instruction](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326943635)

- **Show Review Date** - Turn this on to show the date each review was posted.
- **Show QR Code** - Turn this on to show a QR code on the wall.
- **QR Code** - Where viewers go when they scan the QR code:
- **Google Place – Write Review** - Opens Google so they can write a review of your place.
- **Google Reviews** - Opens your place's Google reviews.
- **Google Map** - Opens your place in Google Maps.
- **Custom** - Choose a QR code from your Files/Assets, such as one that opens your website, a menu or a survey. [Learn how to create a QR Code](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039264133).
- **Leave a Review Instruction** - A short message shown above the QR code, such as **Scan to review**.

<a id="DisplayOptions"></a>

### Display Options

Click **Display Options** to change how the wall looks:

![Display Options section expanded with Theme, Font Size and Speed set to Custom](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326944531)

- **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**, or choose **Custom** and set your own **Background primary**, **Background secondary**, **Text primary** and **Text secondary** colors. Enter a hex code or click the swatch to use the color picker.
- **Background Image** - Keep **None**, or choose **Custom** and hit **Choose** to use an image from your Files/Assets.
- **Font Size** - The size of the review text. Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** and enter your own size.
- **Speed** - How fast the wall moves between reviews. Choose from **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or choose **Custom** and drag the slider.

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded with Filter Type set to By Time and Always Show New Reviews on](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326945299)

- **Filter Type** - Keep **None** to show all reviews, or choose **By Time** and enter a number of days in **Show Reviews From** to only show reviews from the latest days.
- **Only Show Reviews With Text** - Turn this on to hide reviews that only have a star rating.
- **Scrolling Strip Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** and enter your own size for the scrolling strip text.
- **Always Show New Reviews** - Keep this on to show new reviews on your screens as soon as they come in. Turn it off to approve new reviews yourself before they appear. See [Moderating Reviews](#ModeratingReviews) below.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| If none of your reviews have text, turning on **Only Show Reviews With Text** may leave the wall empty. |

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created a Google Reviews app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**.

![New Google Reviews asset shown in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341586707)

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| It may take a few seconds for the app to fetch your reviews. Once saved, click the app in **Files/Assets** to see a live **Preview** of your wall as you change its settings. |

<a id="ModeratingReviews"></a>

### Moderating Reviews

To choose which reviews appear on your screens, click your Google Reviews app in **Files/Assets** and open **Advanced**. Turn off **Always Show New Reviews**, then click the **Moderation** button next to it.

![Advanced section with Always Show New Reviews off and the Moderation button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326948371)

The **Moderation** window will open, listing your reviews. Search for a review or sort the list, then use the buttons on each review to hide it or remove it from your wall.

![Moderation window listing reviews, each with hide and delete buttons](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341590675)

<a id="UsingSignInWithGoogle"></a>

### Using Sign in with Google

Instead of **Enter Public Place**, you can select **Sign in with Google** when creating your Google Reviews app. Sign in with the Google account that manages your business's Google Business Profile, and your business's place will be filled in for you.

![Google Reviews dialog with the Sign in with Google button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326950675)

The rest of the settings are the same as above.

## Deploying a Google Reviews App

To push your Google Reviews app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326951699)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499341593491)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Google Reviews app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Google Reviews asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56499326953875)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Google reviews will now display on your screen.

Your Google Reviews app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
