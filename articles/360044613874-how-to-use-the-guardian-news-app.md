# How to Use the Guardian News App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044613874-How-to-Use-the-Guardian-News-App
Last updated: 2026-09-10T09:42:15Z

### In this article, we'll explain how to use the Guardian News app on OptiSigns.

- [What You'll Need](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040991534#WhatYouNeed)
- [Creating a The Guardian News Asset](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040991534#Creating)

The OptiSigns The Guardian app allows you to display news from The Guardian on your digital signs. It covers the most up-to-date Top Stories, World news, Football, Sport, Culture, Lifestyle news and more.

QR Codes are displayed, which a viewer can scan with their phone to read the relevant article. The Guardian News can also be displayed as [Scrolling Strip.](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559613)

## Creating a The Guardian News Asset

First, you will need to have your screens set up and paired. For more information on how to do that, click [here](https://www.optisigns.com/blog/how-to-set-up-digital-signs-with-optisigns-and-amazon-fire-tv).

Then log on to our portal: <http://app.optisigns.com/>

Go to **Files/Assets**, click on **Apps:**

![OptiSigns portal with the Files/Assets tab and the Apps button in the left sidebar highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263751984915)

Click **The Guardian**:

![Add App dialog with Guardian typed in the search box and The Guardian app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263751991955)

Enter your The Guardian Wall App information:

![The Guardian Wall setup dialog: Name, Category, Theme, Speed and Font Size, with a live preview pane](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263742657683)

- Name: Name of your The Guardian Wall app, this is the name of the app in your asset list. It will **not** be displayed on your screens.
- Category: Select the category or categories that you want to focus on. You can check as many as you want.
- Theme: Select Light or Dark Theme.
- Speed: Select how fast you want the app to scroll or switch between posts. You can also customize your speed if you select Custom.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a slider from 1 to 20 marked Slow, Medium and Fast](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263742658195)

- Font Size: Set the font size for The Guardian Wall app.

Clicking **Advanced**opens up additional options:

![Advanced section expanded, showing the Date & Time and Scrolling Strip Font Size dropdowns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263751997459)

- Date & Time: Choose from a set of pre-formatted dates, or select a custom date format:

  ![Date & Time set to Custom, with a free-text format box beside the dropdown](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55263742666771)

- Scrolling Strip Font Size: If you put the The Guardian News Wall app to a SplitScreen zone that is a scrolling strip, you can set how big you want the scrolling text to be.

After you've configured the Guardian News app to your liking, hit **Save**.

Your Guardian app will appear as an Asset in OptiSigns, and it can now be directly assigned to a screen or used in a Playlist. This asset can then be edited at any time from the Files/Assets tab.
