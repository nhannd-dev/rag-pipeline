## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns account - any plan

<a id="InstagramOrInstagramBusiness"></a>

## Instagram or Instagram Business?

OptiSigns has two Instagram apps. Pick the one that matches what you want to show:

- **Instagram** - Shows the posts from **your own** Instagram profile. You sign in with Instagram directly. This is the app covered in this article.
- **Instagram Business** - Shows the posts from **any public profile** or a **hashtag**. You connect through Facebook, using an Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page. See [How to Use the Instagram Business App](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051495193).

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Both apps need an Instagram **Business** or **Creator** account. Personal accounts can't be used, as this is Meta's policy. Switching is free and only takes a few clicks ([see how](https://help.instagram.com/502981923235522)). |

## Creating an Instagram App

From the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets** and select **Apps**.

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Apps button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500047983891)

Search for **Instagram** and select the **Instagram** app.

![Add App dialog searched for Instagram, with the Instagram app card highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500047987603)

Click **Sign in with Instagram** and sign in with your Instagram account.

![Instagram dialog with the Sign in with Instagram button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500033891603)

The Instagram app settings will open, showing the account you signed in with. Fill in the following:

- **Name** - The name of your Instagram asset. This is for organizational purposes within OptiSigns only and will not display on your screens.
- **Display Mode** - How your posts are laid out. Choose from **Social Kiosk View**, [**Single Post View**](https://social-player.optisigns.com/instagram/?asset_id=y89t3dg76jewc7nec), [**Social Wall View**](https://social-player.optisigns.com/instagram/?asset_id=ry2oyv3hqui83njer), **Timeline View**, **Bricks View**, **Modern View** or **Flip Card View**.
- **Theme** - Choose **Light Theme** or **Dark Theme**, or **Custom** to pick your own colors.
- **Speed** - How fast the app moves between posts. Choose **Slow**, **Medium** or **Fast**, or **Custom** to set your own speed.
- **Font Size** - Keep **Default**, or choose **Custom** to set your own size.

![Instagram app form with the Name, Display Mode, Theme, Speed and Font Size fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500033893267)

When **Theme** is set to **Custom**, enter a hex code for the **Background Color** and **Text Color**, or click the swatch to use the color picker.

![Theme set to Custom, showing the Background Color and Text Color fields](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500047994003)

When **Speed** is set to **Custom**, drag the slider to choose a speed from 1 to 20.

![Speed set to Custom, showing a slider from 1 to 20](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500047994515)

<a id="Advanced"></a>

### Advanced

Click **Advanced** for more options:

![Advanced section expanded with Filter Posts set to By Quantity, showing the moderation, playlist and QR code options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500047997715)

- **Always show new posts** - On by default, so new posts appear automatically. Turn it off to approve posts before they show. See [Moderating Posts](#ModeratingPosts) below.
- **Filter Posts** - Choose **None**, **By Quantity** to show only the latest number of posts you set, or **By Time** to show only posts from the last number of days you set.
- **Only show posts with image** - Turn this on to hide posts without an image.
- **Continue Playing Content in Playlist** - On by default. In a Playlist, the app picks up where it left off each time it comes around, instead of starting again from the first post.
- **Show QR Code** - Turn this on to show a QR code linking to each post.

Now hit **Save**. You'll have created an Instagram app, and it will appear in your **Files/Assets**. It may take a few seconds for the app to fetch your posts.

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| Click **Preview** at the bottom of the app settings to see your wall in a new tab. |

<a id="ModeratingPosts"></a>

### Moderating Posts

To choose which posts appear on your screens, turn off **Always show new posts**, then click the gear button next to it.

![Advanced section with Always show new posts off and the moderation gear button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500048000147)

The **Moderation** window will open, listing your posts. Use the buttons on each post to hide it or remove it from your wall, and click **Refresh Data** to load your newest posts. Learn more about [moderating your social media feeds](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403015887763).

## Deploying an Instagram App

To push your Instagram app to a screen, go to the **Screens** tab, then click **Edit** next to the screen you want to display it on.

![Screens list with the Edit button highlighted on a screen row](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500048000531)

The **Edit Screen** pop-up will appear. Select **Asset** under **Content Type**, then hit **Change** next to **Selected Asset**.

![Edit Screen dialog with Content Type set to Asset and the Change button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500033906323)

The **Select Asset** window will open. Search for your Instagram app, click it, then hit **Select**.

![Select Asset dialog with the Instagram asset selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56500048002451)

Back in **Edit Screen**, hit **Save**. Your Instagram posts will now display on your screen.

Your Instagram app can also be displayed in a Playlist or Schedule, or as one zone of a [Split Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
