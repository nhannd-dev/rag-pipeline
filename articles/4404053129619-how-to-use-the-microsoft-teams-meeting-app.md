# How to Use the Microsoft Teams Meeting App

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404053129619-How-to-Use-the-Microsoft-Teams-Meeting-App
Last updated: 2026-10-01T19:33:01Z

You can broadcast a Microsoft Teams meeting on your digital signs with OptiSigns.

It's simple: create (or schedule) a Microsoft Teams meeting.

In the Meeting Options:

![Microsoft Teams meeting More actions menu open, arrow on Meeting options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/4404053182483)

Make sure you set "Who can bypass the lobby" to "Everyone".

Otherwise, the OptiSigns app will be stuck in the lobby.

![Teams Meeting options panel with Who can bypass the lobby set to Everyone](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/4404053190163)

After that, copy your MS Teams meeting URL.

There are many ways you can copy the MS Teams Meeting URL. Here's one:
Click Participants, then click Share Invite. Then click Copy Link.

![Teams Participants panel, arrows on the Participants icon and Share invite](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/4404053323411)

Log in to OptiSigns:
<https://app.optisigns.com/app/assetManagement>

Click **Files/Assets -> Apps**, type "Teams Meeting" in the search box, and select Microsoft Teams Meeting

![OptiSigns Files/Assets page with the Add App dialog showing Microsoft Teams Meeting](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55993837023507)

Give your MS Teams Meeting app a name and paste the URL in there:

![Microsoft Teams Meeting dialog with the Name and URL fields filled in](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55993852558227)

That's it!

![New Microsoft Teams Meeting asset shown first in Files/Assets](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/55993852559123)

Now you can assign this MS Teams Meeting app to your screens, or put it on a schedule so the screens will join automatically when the meeting starts.

The screens will join the meeting as guests, using the screen names as their display names.

This can also be done with Teams Town hall. For a Town hall, click **Share event**, then copy the **Join link** URL and paste this into OptiSigns.

![Teams Town hall Share event dialog, arrow on the Join link copy button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/38297330621459)

**Tips:**

Have the host start the meeting a few minutes before the screens join automatically.
It will take a device 10-15s to join the meeting, so it's not a good idea to put the Teams Meeting in a playlist. It's better to use it as single asset to take up full screen or be part of a [Split Screen zone](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026559573).
